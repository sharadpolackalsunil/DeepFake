\section{Pre-Registered Statistical Hypotheses and Audits}
\label{sec:stats}

To prevent opportunistic metric reporting and establish rigorous inferential validity, we evaluate three pre-registered statistical hypotheses governing architectural utility, operating point transfer, and operational prior shift.

\subsection{Hypothesis H1: Paired Cluster Bootstrap $\Delta\text{AUC}$ and Equivalence Testing}
\paragraph{Formulation.}
For any model pair $(A, B)$, we evaluate whether model $A$ is statistically superior to model $B$ beyond an equivalence margin $\delta = 0.01$:
\begin{align}
H_0: \Delta\text{AUC} \le \delta \quad \text{vs.} \quad H_1: \Delta\text{AUC} > \delta
\end{align}
We compute 95\% two-sided confidence intervals using $N_{\text{boot}}=2000$ iterations of the \textbf{Paired Cluster Bootstrap}. Crucially, bootstrap sampling is performed at the \emph{cluster level} rather than across individual video clips, resampling 140 video clusters on FF++, 124 celebrity identity clusters on Celeb-DF v2, and 413 identity clusters on DFD. This guarantees cluster-correlated errors between identical actors or underlying source sequences do not artificially inflate statistical significance.

\paragraph{Statistical Conclusions (H1).}
As detailed in Table~\ref{tab:h1_bootstrap}:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Heads Do Not Reliably Help (H1 Supported):} The pre-registered confirmatory comparison tests whether adding temporal attention (\texttt{smsdt\_st}) or full forensic residual streams (\texttt{smsdt\_full}) outperforms single-frame LayerNorm tuning (\texttt{smsdt\_s}) by $\delta = 0.01$. On in-domain FF++, \texttt{smsdt\_st} vs \texttt{smsdt\_s} is strictly \textbf{\texttt{equivalent}} ($[-0.0066, +0.0073]$). Out-of-domain on Celeb-DF v2 and DFD, the point estimates are negative ($-0.0122$ and $-0.0061$, both inconclusive), demonstrating that extra temporal heads do not provide a statistically reliable generalization advantage over single-frame PEFT.
    \item \textbf{Exploratory Trend for High-Frequency Residuals:} Comparing \texttt{smsdt\_full} against \texttt{smsdt\_st} on Celeb-DF v2 yields $\Delta\text{AUC} = \mathbf{+0.0178}$ with a 95\% CI of $[+0.0055, +0.0325]$. While the lower bound is strictly positive ($>0$), because $\text{ci\_lower} = 0.0055 < \delta = 0.01$, the pre-registered equivalence criterion classifies this effect as inconclusive rather than confirmatory.
    \item \textbf{Recipe vs. Architecture Disentanglement (Gate G1 Branch B):} The comparison between \texttt{smsdt\_full} and \texttt{gend\_ours} shows a substantial delta ($+0.0582$ on FF++, $+0.0588$ on Celeb-DF v2). Under pre-registered Gate G1, because our in-house GenD reproduction underperformed published figures ($0.829$ vs $0.946$), this contrast serves as an internal ablation highlighting the critical role of optimization recipe and loss formulation under identical pretrained backbones.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Hypothesis H2: Operating Point Transfer and Degradation Gap $g$}
\paragraph{Formulation.}
When deploying a deepfake detector, the decision threshold $\tau_{\text{val}}$ must be fixed prior to deployment (e.g., via Youden's $J$ statistic on validation data). Hypothesis H2 pre-registered that validation-frozen operating cutoffs would experience catastrophic operating point failure under cross-dataset domain shift, formulated as a degradation gap:
\begin{equation}
g = \text{HTER}(\tau_{\text{val}}) - \min_{\tau} \text{HTER}(\tau) \ge 0.05
\end{equation}
exceeding 5.0\% HTER on unseen evaluation datasets.

\paragraph{Audit Results (H2 Falsified).}
As reported across all individual runs in Table~\ref{tab:master_matrix}, the empirical degradation gap remains uniformly small across all 36 evaluated conditions:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item In-domain FF++: $g \in [0.0000, 0.0121]$ (average $0.0061$).
    \item Cross-dataset Celeb-DF v2: $g \in [0.0029, 0.0374]$ (average $0.0118$).
    \item Cross-dataset DFD: $g \in [0.0003, 0.0173]$ (average $0.0053$).
\end{itemize}
Because $g \le 0.0374 \ll 0.05$ across every condition, Hypothesis H2 is formally \textbf{falsified}. This falsification delivers an important scientific insight: cross-dataset operational errors are not primarily driven by threshold miscalibration ($g$ is negligible), but rather by rank-order score overlap and false positive rate inflation on authentic out-of-domain faces.

\subsection{Hypothesis H3: Operational Prior Shift and Saerens EM Correction}
\paragraph{Formulation.}
In production environments, the base rate of synthetic content $\pi = P(Y=1)$ deviates unpredictably from training class balance ($0.50$). Hypothesis H3 evaluates detector resilience under simulated prior shifts $\pi \in \{0.1, 0.3, 0.5, 0.7, 0.9\}$ on Celeb-DF v2 (50 Monte Carlo resamplings per condition) and tests whether unsupervised Saerens Expectation-Maximization (EM) \citep{saerens2002adjusting} accurately estimates the true operational prior:
\begin{equation}
p^{(k+1)}(Y=1|x) \propto \frac{\pi^{(k)}}{0.50} \cdot p(Y=1|x)
\end{equation}

Furthermore, across the operational band $\pi \in [0.30, 0.70]$, frozen validation cutoffs maintain stable error boundaries ($19.1\% - 19.8\%$ HTER), demonstrating high practical resilience against shifting traffic distributions.

\subsection{Test-Time Adaptation (TTA) and Equilibrium Collapse Audit}
\label{sec:tta_audit}
\paragraph{Formulation.}
To investigate whether unsupervised test-time optimization can repair transferred representations without labels, we evaluate LayerNorm entropy minimization \citep{wang2021tent} at learning rate $\eta = 10^{-4}$. We compare:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Online Streaming TTA:} Continuous adaptation where parameters update sequentially across the unseen test video stream.
    \item \textbf{Batch-Episodic TTA:} Parameters are updated strictly on the current test mini-batch, predictions are recorded, and LayerNorm weights are immediately reset to their source state.
\end{enumerate}
Following pre-registration, we flag an operating failure as \emph{Equilibrium Collapse} if post-adaptation AUC degrades by $> 0.03$ or if predicted positive share diverges to $>95\%$ or $<5\%$ under balanced ground truth.

\paragraph{Empirical Findings.}
As revealed in Table~\ref{tab:tta_benchmark}, unconstrained online entropy minimization suffers catastrophic failure on deepfake test streams. In online mode, prediction entropy declines rapidly, driving logits into a degenerate trivial attractor where nearly all test samples are classified as authentic ($6.6\%$ positive on Celeb-DF, $0.6\%$ on DFD). Introducing our multi-clip consistency objective (H6) moderates the magnitude of drift ($0.5409$ vs $0.3386$ AUC), yet small-batch streaming without episodic reset ($B=2$ videos over 259 steps) still exhibits equilibrium collapse. In contrast, \textbf{Batch-Episodic} adaptation prevents error accumulation across both objectives, preserving discrimination ($0.8877$ post-update consistency AUC vs $0.8880$ static) while tracking dataset class prevalence ($60.4\%$ vs $65.6\%$).

\subsection{Hypotheses H4 \& H5: False Positive Rate Control Under Shift}
\label{sec:fpr_control}
\paragraph{Formulation.}
While HTER changes remain small under domain shift, aggregate HTER averages false positive rate (FPR) and false negative rate (FNR), masking catastrophic spikes in false alarms on pristine authentic faces. Hypothesis H4 posits that freezing a source-domain threshold targeting $\alpha = 5\%$ FPR results in severe FPR inflation ($> 2\alpha = 10\%$) when deployed on unseen authentic domains, particularly for high-capacity heads. Hypothesis H5 tests whether unsupervised 2-component Gaussian Mixture thresholding (\texttt{target\_gmm}) recovers the target $\alpha$ budget without target labels.

\paragraph{Audit Conclusions (H4 Confirmed, H5 Falsified).}
As shown in Table~\ref{tab:fpr_control}, single-frame tuning (\texttt{smsdt\_s}) transfers closest to the target 5\% budget ($6.7\% - 10.7\%$ achieved FPR). Scaling trainable capacity to spatio-temporal (\texttt{smsdt\_st}) and dual-stream (\texttt{smsdt\_full}) heads causes systematic false-alarm inflation reaching $16.9\% - 22.5\%$ (all lower 95\% CI bounds strictly exceed $2\alpha = 10\%$), confirming \textbf{H4}. Furthermore, label-free 2-component GMM thresholding (\texttt{target\_gmm} and \texttt{target\_gmm\_anchored}) fails to satisfy H5 at both $\alpha = 0.05$ and $\alpha = 0.10$: on fake-skewed test streams, EM shifts cutoffs excessively high on high-capacity models (collapsing \texttt{smsdt\_full} recall to $15.0\% - 15.9\%$ on Celeb-DF v2 and $0.0\%$ on DFD at $\alpha = 0.05$) while still breaching upper FPR CI bounds on \texttt{gend\_ours} and \texttt{smsdt\_st}.

\subsection{Perturbation Robustness Under Spatial Corruptions}
\label{sec:robustness_audit}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
